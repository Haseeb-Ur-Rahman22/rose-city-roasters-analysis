# Client Discovery Brief

*This is a discovery brief written in the business owner's voice, the kind of conversation that starts a real project. It captures what the owner is worried about and what they want answered, before any analysis begins. The business here is a demonstration case (a specialty coffee roaster) built to show how Basirah approaches a real engagement.*

---

## About the business

We roast and sell specialty coffee. We started as a single cafe and grew into something bigger: a few cafe locations, an online store, and a wholesale side where we supply beans and equipment to other cafes and restaurants. We've been at this for about four years now.

On paper, things look fine. Sales keep going up year after year. The cafes are busy. Wholesale keeps signing new accounts. So for a while I didn't think we had a problem.

## What's bothering me

Here's the thing that keeps me up at night: **we're selling more than ever, but there never seems to be more money in the bank.** Revenue is up, but it doesn't feel like we're actually getting ahead. Some months are tight even though the sales numbers look great.

I have a few specific worries but no way to prove or disprove them:

- **I think some of our products might be losing money** and I don't even know which ones. Coffee prices have gone up a lot, and I'm not sure our prices kept up. Are we selling some things for less than they cost us?
- **We discount a lot.** Promotions, wholesale deals, loyalty perks. It feels good to move product, but I have no idea how much those discounts are actually costing us.
- **Our wholesale customers are slow to pay.** I feel like I'm always chasing invoices. But I don't know if that's a real pattern or just a few bad accounts I remember because they annoyed me.

I don't have a data team. I have spreadsheets and a point-of-sale system that spits out transactions. I need someone to look at all of it and tell me the truth.

## What I want to know

1. **Are we actually making money, and if not, why not?** Not just revenue, the real profit after everything.
2. **Which products or categories are draining profit?** I want a list I can act on.
3. **How much are discounts really costing us?** Is it worth it?
4. **Which customers pay late, and is it a real problem?** I want to know where the risk actually is, not my gut feeling.

## What I want out of it

Plain answers, not a data science lecture. Show me where the money is leaking and tell me what to do about it. If there are a few clear actions I can take to fix this, that's what I care about. A dashboard I can keep looking at would be a bonus, but the first thing I need is clarity.
